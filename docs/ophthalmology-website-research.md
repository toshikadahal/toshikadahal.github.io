## Patterns used

- Clear doctor identity in the first screen
- Subspecialty focus in short phrases
- Appointment call to action above the fold
- Parent friendly eye topic cards
- Red flag symptoms separated from routine advice
- Doctor credentials separated from patient education
- Soft hospital color palette
- Mobile first layout
- Dark and light theme support
- SEO terms for pediatric ophthalmology, strabismus, squint, lazy eye, and Nepal
