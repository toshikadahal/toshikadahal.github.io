Operations and treatment information

Clear information before ENT procedures

This section explains common ENT operations in simple language so patients can understand why surgery is advised, what area is treated, and what questions to ask during consultation.

Ear operations

Tympanoplasty for repair of tympanic membrane perforation and improvement of middle ear health.

Mastoidectomy for selected chronic ear disease and mastoid infection.

Myringotomy and ventilation tube insertion for selected middle ear fluid and recurrent ear problems.

Discussion of hearing surgery options when conductive hearing loss, ossicular problems, or otosclerosis are suspected.

Nose and sinus operations

Septoplasty for deviated nasal septum when nasal blockage affects breathing or quality of life.

Functional endoscopic sinus surgery for selected chronic sinusitis, nasal polyps, and blocked sinus drainage pathways.

Nasal endoscopy based evaluation for chronic blockage, allergy, sinus symptoms, and recurrent nasal discharge.

Epistaxis care for recurrent nosebleeds after identifying local and systemic risk factors.

Throat and voice operations

Tonsillectomy or adenoid related care for selected recurrent infection, snoring, obstruction, and sleep related symptoms.

Microlaryngeal evaluation for persistent hoarseness, vocal fold lesions, and voice change.

Throat symptom evaluation for reflux related irritation, swallowing symptoms, and warning signs.

Head and neck operations

Thyroid swelling evaluation with clinical examination, imaging review, and surgical planning when needed.

Salivary gland swelling and stone related evaluation with conservative and surgical care pathways.

Parotid and submandibular gland assessment for lumps, infection, and selected surgical conditions.

Neck lump evaluation with attention to duration, growth, pain, infection, voice change, swallowing difficulty, and red flag symptoms.

For patients

When should ENT symptoms be checked?

Persistent, recurrent, painful, progressive, or worrying symptoms should be assessed. ENT review is especially important when symptoms affect hearing, breathing, swallowing, voice, balance, sleep, or daily function.

Seek urgent care for sudden hearing loss, breathing difficulty, bleeding, facial weakness, severe pain, or rapidly increasing neck swelling.

Avoid unprescribed ear drops, oil, or instruments inside the ear.

Bring old prescriptions, hearing tests, imaging reports, and previous operation notes to consultation.

Children with persistent mouth breathing, snoring, recurrent ear infection, delayed speech, or hearing concerns need proper evaluation.

Clinic approach

Examination, imaging review, and follow up

Good ENT care depends on careful history, targeted examination, proper use of endoscopy or microscopy when needed, and clear explanation of the treatment plan.

Endoscopic assessment

Nasal, throat, and laryngeal symptoms may need endoscopic evaluation to identify the affected area clearly.

Microscopic ear care

Ear discharge, wax, perforation, chronic ear disease, and post operative ear care may need microscopic evaluation.

Hearing and vestibular pathway

Hearing loss, tinnitus, dizziness, and balance symptoms may need audiology or vestibular testing depending on the clinical findings.

Imaging review

CT, MRI, ultrasound, or X ray reports should be reviewed in the clinical context before treatment or surgery decisions.
