Smell loss

Smell loss is assessed by timing, nasal blockage, sinus symptoms, viral illness, head injury, medicine use, and whether taste is also affected.

01

Common symptoms

Reduced smell

Distorted smell

Loss of taste perception

Nasal blockage

Post viral onset

02

How ENT evaluation may be done

Nasal examination

Endoscopy for obstruction or polyps

Sinus assessment

CT or MRI in selected cases

Medication and neurological history

03

Treatment options

Treating rhinitis or sinusitis

Steroid spray when indicated

Smell training discussion

Polyp treatment if present

Referral if neurological features exist

04

Home care and preparation

Use smoke and gas safety alarms

Check food expiry carefully

Avoid unnecessary strong irritants

Track gradual recovery

05

Questions to ask during consultation

Is my smell loss blocked nose related?

Do I have polyps?

Is imaging needed?

Can smell training help?

Warning signs

When to seek urgent care

Smell loss with neurological symptoms, severe headache, trauma, or one sided nasal bleeding needs prompt evaluation.
