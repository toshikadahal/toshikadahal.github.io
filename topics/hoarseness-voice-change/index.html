Voice

Hoarseness and voice change

Hoarseness can result from infection, voice misuse, reflux, vocal fold nodules, polyps, cysts, paralysis, thyroid surgery related nerve issues, or cancer. Examination of the larynx is important when symptoms persist.

Common symptoms

Rough voice

Breathy voice

Voice fatigue

Pain while speaking

Loss of voice range

How ENT evaluation may be done

Throat history

Flexible laryngoscopy

Voice use assessment

Reflux and allergy review

Imaging or biopsy discussion for suspicious lesions

Treatment options

Voice rest for acute strain

Hydration and voice hygiene

Reflux management when appropriate

Voice therapy referral

Microlaryngeal surgery for selected lesions

Home care and preparation

Avoid shouting

Avoid whispering when voice is strained

Hydrate well

Rest the voice after heavy use

Questions to ask during consultation

Are my vocal folds moving normally?

Do I need voice therapy?

Is reflux contributing?

Is there any lesion?

Warning signs

Hoarseness with breathing difficulty, blood, swallowing difficulty, neck lump, weight loss, or persistence beyond a few weeks needs evaluation.
