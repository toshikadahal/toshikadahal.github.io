Ear

Otosclerosis and conductive hearing loss

Conductive hearing loss means sound is not reaching the inner ear efficiently. Otosclerosis is one possible cause where stapes movement is reduced.

Common symptoms

Gradual hearing loss

Better hearing in noisy environments in some cases

Tinnitus

Normal looking eardrum in some patients
