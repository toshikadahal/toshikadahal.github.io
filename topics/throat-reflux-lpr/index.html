Throat

Throat reflux and LPR symptoms

Laryngopharyngeal reflux can mimic sinus drip, allergy, voice strain, and throat sensitivity. Diagnosis is clinical and should avoid assuming all throat symptoms are reflux.

01

Common symptoms

Throat clearing

Chronic cough

Lump sensation

Voice change

Burning or sour taste in some patients

Treatment options

Diet and meal timing advice

Weight and lifestyle measures

Voice hygiene

Medication trial when appropriate

Evaluation for other causes if symptoms persist

04

Home care and preparation

Avoid late heavy meals

Reduce smoking and alcohol exposure

Hydrate well

Avoid excessive throat clearing

When to seek urgent care
