Throat

Tonsils, adenoids, snoring, and sleep

Tonsil and adenoid problems can affect infection frequency, sleep quality, breathing, school performance, growth, and ear health. Surgery is considered only after assessing severity and benefit.

Warning signs

When to seek urgent care

Breathing difficulty, dehydration, severe one sided throat pain, neck swelling, or suspected abscess needs urgent care.
