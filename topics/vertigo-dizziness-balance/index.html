Balance

Vertigo, dizziness, and balance

Dizziness may be caused by inner ear disorders, BPPV, vestibular neuritis, Meniere type symptoms, migraine, blood pressure changes, medicine effects, anxiety, or neurological disease.

Common symptoms

Room spinning

Nausea or vomiting

Imbalance while walking

Hearing loss or tinnitus

Headache or neurological symptoms

How ENT evaluation may be done

Detailed timing and trigger history

Ear and neurological screening

Positional tests when BPPV is suspected

Hearing test when ear symptoms are present

Referral for vestibular testing or imaging when needed

Treatment options

Canalith repositioning for BPPV

Medicines for acute severe symptoms

Vestibular exercises

Treatment of inner ear or migraine related causes

Referral if central features are suspected

Home care and preparation

Avoid driving during severe vertigo

Hydrate well

Rise slowly from bed

Record attack duration and triggers

Warning signs

Emergency care is needed for dizziness with weakness, double vision, slurred speech, severe headache, chest pain, fainting, or inability to walk.
