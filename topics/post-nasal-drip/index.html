Post nasal drip and throat clearing

Post nasal drip is a common reason for throat clearing, cough, irritation, and a lump sensation. Treatment depends on the cause rather than the symptom alone.

Common symptoms

Throat clearing

Mucus sensation

Cough

Blocked nose or discharge

Warning signs

Persistent throat symptoms with weight loss, blood, swallowing difficulty, voice change, or neck lump need prompt evaluation.
