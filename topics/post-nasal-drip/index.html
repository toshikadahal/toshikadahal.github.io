Post nasal drip and throat clearing

Post nasal drip and throat clearing can have more than one cause, so careful history, examination, and follow-up help guide safe treatment.

Post nasal drip is a common reason for throat clearing, cough, irritation, and a lump sensation. Treatment depends on the cause rather than the symptom alone.
