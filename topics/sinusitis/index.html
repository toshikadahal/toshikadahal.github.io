Sinusitis and facial pressure

Facial pressure and nasal symptoms may have several causes, so treatment works best when the underlying pattern is clear.

Sinusitis may be acute, recurrent, or chronic. Chronic symptoms need careful review because allergy, polyps, dental disease, reflux, migraine, and anatomical blockage can mimic or worsen sinus complaints.

01

Common symptoms

Facial pressure

Thick nasal discharge

Post nasal drip

Smell reduction

Cough or throat clearing

02

How ENT evaluation may be done

History of duration and recurrence

Nasal endoscopy

Allergy and polyp assessment

CT sinus for selected chronic cases

Dental review if dental source is suspected

03

Treatment options

Saline irrigation

Nasal steroid spray

Antibiotics only when indicated

Allergy management

Functional endoscopic sinus surgery when medical treatment fails in selected cases

04

Home care and preparation

Use saline irrigation safely

Avoid smoke exposure

Complete prescribed medicine correctly

Bring CT reports if already done

05

Questions to ask during consultation

Is this sinusitis or migraine?

Do I have polyps?

Do I need CT?

What is the aim of sinus surgery?

Warning signs

When to seek urgent care

Severe headache, eye swelling, double vision, confusion, neck stiffness, or high fever needs urgent care.
