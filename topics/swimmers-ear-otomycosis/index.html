Ear

Swimmer's ear and otomycosis

Swimmer's ear and otomycosis can have more than one cause, so careful history, examination, and follow-up help guide safe treatment.

Otitis externa and fungal otitis are infections or inflammation of the ear canal. Moisture, scratching, wax removal attempts, diabetes, and ear devices may increase risk.

01

Common symptoms

Ear pain

Itching

Discharge

Blocked ear

Pain when touching the ear

02

How ENT evaluation may be done

Ear canal examination

Microscopic cleaning

Assessment of eardrum

Fungal debris identification

Culture in recurrent cases

03

Treatment options

Aural toilet

Topical drops

Keeping ear dry

Avoiding trauma to canal

Diabetes or immunity review in recurrent severe cases

04

Home care and preparation

Keep the ear dry

Avoid scratching

Avoid ear buds

Do not share earphones during infection

Warning signs

When to seek urgent care

Severe pain, swelling, fever, diabetes with worsening ear infection, facial weakness, or spreading redness needs urgent care.
